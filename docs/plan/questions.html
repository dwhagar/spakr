Open questions and defaults chosen

Where the brief left a choice open, a default was picked so planning could continue. The six main questions have been answered (below).

MySQL as the truth writes about 15 times faster but cannot rebuild itself, is 7 times larger in backups and cannot carry parity in place (RES.29)

Still open

New open questions

  1. MultiMarkdown: accept a MultiMarkdown-compatible dialect on Python-Markdown (recommended, in-process, safe, maintained) or require the real MultiMarkdown 6 engine built from source on every LTS? The default above is the dialect. See RES.1.
  2. Minimum password length: 15 characters (recommended; current NIST guidance when a password is the only sign-in method, with no composition rules), 12, or 8 with optional one-time-code two-factor sign-in added later? The default above is 15.
  3. Secrets in backups (RES.31): exclude secrets and back up a redacted config.json, re-entering secrets on restore (recommended), include config.json unencrypted at mode 0600, or encrypt every archive (age or gpg)? The plan assumes the recommended option, with optional encryption for the off-host copy.

Earlier questions: Resolved late: video limit 100 MB; originals kept privately after transcoding with an admin purge option.

Also decided: Flask API-first architecture, API keys, Markdown/media storage layout, caching, accounts and SEO as planned in platform services.
