Dependency tree

Everything starts at the left of the ladder and at the roots of the tree. Phase 0.0 is research and decisions (RES) plus the plan and process; 0.1 is the foundation that nearly everything uses; the later phases stack on top, ending in 0.9, the pre-release working version, and then 1.0. Phases 0.1 to 0.8 are building blocks.

Item tree

Each item sits under the item it most directly builds on (its latest, deepest dependency). If it needs more, the extra ids are shown as "also needs". The version tag is the phase that delivers it. Full item lists with their needs are on the phases page. Items with no dependencies are the roots. 154 items.
