Saltmarch Coast

  • place
  • high fantasy
  • coast

The Saltmarch is a long, fog-bound coast of tidal flats, drowned villages and lighthouses that nobody remembers lighting. Travellers cross it on the Causeway of Teeth twice a day, between tides.

GM note. Pages marked GM-only never appear for readers; the badge here is shown by more than colour.

Tides and travel

Crossing time depends on the tide. A simple model: t=dv−vc, where d is the distance, v the party's speed and vc the current. Rendered as MathML on the server, so it needs no script and no fonts.
