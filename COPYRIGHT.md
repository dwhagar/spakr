## Requesting removal

If you are a copyright holder, or act for one, and believe material on a spakr site infringes your rights, email **sysop@moltenaether.com** with:

1. a description of the work you say has been infringed;
2. the address (URL) of the page or file you want removed;
3. your name and contact details;
4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law, and that the information in your request is accurate.

We will review requests promptly and remove or disable access to material where appropriate.
